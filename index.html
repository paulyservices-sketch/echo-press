Questions

Before you press play.

Does it actually sound good, or is this a novelty?

Real lacquer cutting, real mastering, real turntable playback. It has the warmth and hiss of genuine vinyl — that's the whole point. It's not a printed sticker on a fake disc.

What audio formats can I send?

MP3, WAV, M4A, or a straight-up phone voice memo. If you can play it out loud, we can press it.

How long does pressing take?

Standard pressing ships in 9–14 business days. Press Pass members get priority 48-hour mastering before the standard queue.

Can I press something explicit or private?

Yes — every record is pressed for one customer and shipped directly to them. We don't publish, stream, or store your audio anywhere public.

What if my record arrives damaged?

Every plan includes free replacement pressing if your record arrives cracked or warped. Just show us a photo and we'll cut a new one.
